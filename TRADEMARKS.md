# Trademarks and attribution

The cards in this repository emulate the appearance and behaviour of
building-systems hardware so that a dashboard reads like the equipment it
represents.

**All product names, model numbers, logos and trade marks are the property of
their respective owners.** Their use here is for identification and
compatibility only.

This project is **independent and unaffiliated**. It is not endorsed by,
sponsored by, or connected with Schneider Electric, Circutor, Daikin, QSC,
Crestron, AMPAC, Tridium, or any other manufacturer whose equipment a
faceplate may resemble.

Where a faceplate emulates a specific product, the `emulates:` field names
it factually, and the card's editor shows that attribution to the user.

## Brand assets

Any logo or wordmark artwork lives under `assets/brand/` and nowhere else, so
that a build without manufacturer marks is a matter of removing one
directory. Faceplates are designed to remain legible and useful without them.

If you are a rights holder and would like an asset removed, open an issue and
it will be taken out.
